The control plane

Build freely.
Connect once.

Your apps keep their own logic and databases. MeteorBase handles the trust boundary, routing, access control, and observability between them.

⌁

One client key

Give developers one revocable key and let them request data from every active service through the same predictable interface.

◈

Service secrets

Each microservice receives a private secret and trusted headers. Requests are verified before they touch your app logic.

⌁

Clear telemetry

Log every route, status, latency, heartbeat, and service state from one admin surface instead of stitching dashboards together.

How it links together

Three layers.
No duplicated auth.

Register a service once, copy the receiver helper into its app, and publish the gateway URL to clients. The hub becomes the only public contract.
